# D’Vaaw Perfume presentation guide

Open [the live website](https://dvaaw-fragrance-my.web.app/) and use this short walkthrough.

1. **Introduce the brand.** Show the parchment, charcoal and gold design, original product photography, Gula Apong story and Sarawak identity.
2. **Find a signature scent.** Select “Find my scent”, answer three questions and explore the suggested perfumes. Suggestions are based on the collection’s listed notes.
3. **Explore the collection.** Search for “roselle”, open Mia Bubblelush and show its description, top/heart/base notes and both bottle sizes.
4. **Save a favourite.** Select the solid heart icon, close the product view and use “Saved scents”. Favourites remain on the same browser/device after refreshing.
5. **Review an order.** Add a perfume, open the bag and choose shipping or pickup. Review the order and use “Keep Editing” during a presentation. Continuing opens WhatsApp and can record a real order request, so only continue for a genuine enquiry.
6. **Show it on mobile.** Demonstrate the menu, product view, scent finder and touch-friendly controls.
7. **Show staff access separately.** Sign in with an approved administrator account. Product, stock, size pricing and discount changes affect the live store.

## Delivery notes

- The site is served over HTTPS by Firebase Hosting. All decorative emoji have been replaced with a shared set of solid SVG icons.
- Checkout is a WhatsApp order request. The customer sends the prepared message; the business confirms stock, payment and fulfilment personally.
- A custom domain still needs to be purchased and connected to Firebase Hosting. The current web.app address works independently.
- TikTok and Facebook currently open searches for the supplied page names. Direct profile links can replace these when the exact URLs are provided.
- This release updates the website only; it does not change Firebase rules or database records.
